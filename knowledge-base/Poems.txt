1. 🌞 The Morning Sun

The morning sun begins to shine,
Its golden rays are warm and fine.
The birds wake up and start to sing,
Flowers smile in early spring.
A brand-new day has now begun.

2. 🌧️ Rainy Day

Raindrops falling from the sky,
Little clouds are passing by.
Puddles sparkle on the ground,
Happy children dance around.
Rain makes everything fresh and bright.

3. 🌳 My Little Tree

I have a little tree,
Growing tall beside me.
It gives me shade each day,
And keeps the heat away.
Birds sit happily on every tree.

4. 🦋 The Little Butterfly

A little butterfly flies so high,
Dancing softly in the sky.
It visits flowers red and blue,
Then flies away when day is through.
What a lovely sight to see!

5. 🐦 Little Bird

A little bird sits on a tree,
Singing a happy song for me.
It spreads its wings and flies away,
To greet the bright and sunny day.
Then it comes home at the end of day.

6. 🌈 The Rainbow

A rainbow shines across the sky,
With colors bright and wide.
Red and yellow, green and blue,
Orange and purple shining too.
It makes the cloudy sky look new.

7. 📚 My School

I go to school each morning bright,
I learn new things and do them right.
I read my books and write each day,
I learn and laugh and learn to play.
My school is where I grow and learn.

8. 🌙 Good Night

The moon is shining in the sky,
Little stars are twinkling high.
The world is quiet, soft, and still,
Dreams are waiting beyond the hill.
Good night, sleep well till morning light.

9. 🌱 Little Seed

A little seed sleeps in the ground,
Waiting for the rain to come around.
The sun shines warm upon its head,
Soon a tiny plant is spread.
One day it will become a tree.

10. 🐝 Busy Little Bee

A busy little bee flies around,
Looking for flowers on the ground.
It visits roses, pink and red,
Then flies back to its honey shed.
Buzz, buzz, buzz, all day long!
