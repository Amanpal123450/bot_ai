Prizes & Claims FAQs

Q: What can I win?
A: Prizes fall into three types, and a single prize tier can combine more than one: gift vouchers (delivered digitally to your registered email), physical gifts (shipped after verification), and free-vote credits (added to your wallet with their own expiry).

Q: How do I claim my prize?
A: After being notified, submit the requested identity documents (Aadhaar/DigiLocker verification and, where required, a short video declaration) within 15 days of the contest ending.

Q: Why wasn't my prize awarded?
A: Common reasons: submitted documents don't match your profile details, the 15-day claim deadline was missed, the account was deleted, or fraud was confirmed during review.

Q: Can I transfer, exchange, or get cash instead of my prize?
A: No — prizes are non-transferable, non-exchangeable, and non-refundable.

Q: My physical prize wasn't delivered / was returned — what now?
A: If a shipped prize is returned as undeliverable, you have 30 calendar days to arrange redelivery or pickup before it's forfeited.

Q: Are prizes different depending on where I live in India?
A: Some prize types may be sub-classified by state due to local regulations. If residence verification doesn't clear for a particular prize, an alternate prize type may be offered instead.
