GRIEVANCE OFFICER POLICY

Q: Who is the Grievance Officer for Kikoo?
A: Kikoo appoints a Grievance Officer to address participant complaints and help ensure fairness, safety, and regulatory compliance.

Q: What complaints can I make to the Kikoo Grievance Officer?
A: Complaints can relate to data privacy and misuse of personal information, unauthorized use of baby photos, contest fairness, fraud, voting manipulation, or violations of Kikoo's Terms & Conditions or Privacy Policy.

Q: How can I contact the Kikoo Grievance Officer?
A: You can contact the Kikoo Grievance Officer by email at grievance@kikoo.in.

Q: What is the address of the Kikoo Grievance Officer?
A: The listed address is Kikoo Baby Contest, New Delhi, India.

Q: How do I submit a grievance to Kikoo?
A: Complaints can be submitted by email or written letter. The complaint should include the participant's name, contact details, a description of the issue, and supporting evidence if available.

Q: How quickly will Kikoo acknowledge my complaint?
A: The Grievance Officer will acknowledge receipt of a complaint within 24 hours.

Q: How long does Kikoo take to resolve a grievance?
A: Kikoo states that a resolution will be provided within 15 working days as per the applicable IT Rules.

Q: What can I do if I am not satisfied with the grievance resolution?
A: If you are not satisfied with the resolution, you may escalate the matter to the relevant regulatory or child protection authorities, depending on the nature of the complaint.

Q: Where can I escalate a regulatory complaint about Kikoo?
A: For regulatory compliance matters, complaints may be escalated to the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), as applicable.

Q: Where can I report an issue involving a child's data or photo?
A: Issues involving misuse of minors' data or unauthorized dissemination of children's photos may be escalated to the relevant child protection authorities.

Q: Does Kikoo maintain records of grievances?
A: Yes. Kikoo states that it maintains a secure log of grievances and resolutions for audit purposes.

Q: Does Kikoo publish grievance reports?
A: Kikoo may publish summary grievance reports annually to demonstrate compliance and accountability.
