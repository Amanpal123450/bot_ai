General FAQs

Q: Is Kikoo free to enter?
A: Yes. Registration and contest participation are always free — purchasing votes is optional and never required to win.

Q: What age groups does Kikoo cover?
A: Categories are organized by age and gender bands (e.g., Baby Girls 7–18 months); exact bands vary by contest and are shown at entry.

Q: Which payment methods are supported?
A: UPI, cards, netbanking, and wallets — via Razorpay and Cashfree, India's leading payment gateways.

Q: Who do I contact for help?
A: Reach our support team through the Contact page — include your registered mobile/email and, if relevant, your payment or contest reference.

Q: Is Kikoo a scam?
A: No. Kikoo is a legitimate photo-contest platform operated by Zolowin, with transparent, backend-verified voting, licensed payment processing, and a published claim and refund policy. See our Terms & Conditions for full details.
