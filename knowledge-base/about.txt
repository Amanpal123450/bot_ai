ABOUT KIKOO

Q: What is Kikoo?
A: Kikoo is an online baby photo contest platform in India. Parents can upload their baby's photo, share it with friends and family, and collect votes. Kikoo hosts contests with real prizes for winners and is designed to be safe and enjoyable for families.

Q: What is Kikoo's mission?
A: Kikoo's mission is to provide a safe, trustworthy, and engaging platform where parents across India can showcase their child's charm, celebrate precious moments, connect with a supportive community, and win rewarding prizes.

Q: Is Kikoo free to enter?
A: Yes. Kikoo contests are 100% free to enter, with zero entry fees.

Q: Who can use Kikoo?
A: Kikoo is designed for families and parents across India. Parents can participate by entering their baby's photo in contests, while friends and family can support participants by voting.

Q: What does Kikoo offer?
A: Kikoo offers monthly baby photo contests, community voting, real prizes, gift vouchers and cash rewards for winners, along with safety and winner verification processes.

Q: How does a Kikoo contest work?
A: Parents enter their baby in a contest by submitting a photo. They can then share the entry with friends and family to collect votes. Contest rankings are based on genuine community votes according to the applicable contest rules.

Q: How are winners verified?
A: Kikoo uses a secure winner verification process, including Aadhaar-based winner verification.

Q: Is Kikoo safe for children?
A: Kikoo prioritizes child privacy and platform safety through photo moderation and protected content safeguards.

Q: Are Kikoo contests fair?
A: Kikoo aims to provide fair and transparent contests. Rankings are driven by genuine community votes and the applicable contest rules.

Q: What prizes can winners receive?
A: Kikoo winners can receive real cash prizes and gift vouchers, according to the applicable contest and prize rules.

Q: How often are contests held?
A: Kikoo runs contests monthly. Users should check the current active contest and its rules for the latest details.

Q: How can I participate in a Kikoo contest?
A: Parents can participate by entering their baby in the active month's contest. Contest entry is 100% free.

Q: Where can I participate?
A: You can participate through Kikoo's website at kikoo.in/participate.
