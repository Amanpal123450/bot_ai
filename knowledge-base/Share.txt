Share FAQs


Q: How do I share my child's vote page?
A: Every entry gets a unique vote link and ready-made share cards from your dashboard — share directly to WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, or copy the link.

Q: sharing help my ranking?
A: Sharing itself doesn't add votes automatically, but it's the main way to bring friends and family to your child's vote page — and some achievements reward active sharing with bonus votes.

Q: Can I customize my share message?
A: Yes, the share card includes an editable caption alongside your child's photo and vote link.
