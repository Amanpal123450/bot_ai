Frequently Asked Questions:

Q: Is Kikoo free to join?
A: Yes, registering and participating in most contests is free unless stated otherwise.

Q: Can I edit my baby's photo after submitting?
A: Photos generally cannot be changed once submitted for a live contest. Contact support if needed.

Q: How do I know if I won?
A: Winners are announced on the contest page and contacted directly via phone or email.

Q: Can I enter multiple contests with the same baby?
A: Yes, as long as each contest allows new entries and your baby meets the age criteria.

Q: How do I contact Kikoo support?
A: You can reach out via the "Contact Us" section on kikoo.in.

Q: What are Kikoo's official social media channels?
A: Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/kikoo_kids/  YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/@kikoo_kidss  web:https://kikoo.in/

Q: What is the main purpose of Kikoo?
A: Kikoo is a baby photo contest platform created to celebrate children, share joyful moments, connect families, and reward winners with prizes.

Q: I want to participate but don't know where to start.
A: You can start by visiting the Participate section on Kikoo and submitting your child's entry. Participation is completely free.

Q: I am a new user, how can I participate?
A: As a new user, you can participate by completing the entry process on Kikoo.

Q: Does Kikoo charge parents any entry fee?
A: No. Kikoo is 100% free to enter. There are no entry or registration fees.
