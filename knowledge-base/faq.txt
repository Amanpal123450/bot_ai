Frequently Asked Questions:

Q: Is Kikoo free to join?
A: Yes, registering and participating in most contests is free unless stated otherwise.

Q: Can I edit my baby's photo after submitting?
A: Photos generally cannot be changed once submitted for a live contest. Contact support if needed.

Q: How do I know if I won?
A: Winners are announced on the contest page and contacted directly via phone or email.

Q: Can I enter multiple contests with the same baby?
A: Yes, as long as each contest allows new entries and your baby meets the age criteria.

Q: How do I contact Kikoo support?
A: You can reach out via the "Contact Us" section on kikoo.in.
